# Transportation News — 2026-10-29

## Autonomous Vehicles
- [Waymo expands to five more US metro areas](https://waymo.com/blog/shorts/more-places/) — Source: Waymo blog; published 2026-10-27. Waymo is growing its fully autonomous ride-hailing footprint in Los Angeles (south to Torrance/South Bay), Nashville, Orlando (nearly doubling eastern coverage), San Diego (north to La Jolla), and the SF Bay Area (south, plus a new BART connection at Berryessa Station), adding roughly 250 square miles combined — continued densification of existing geofences rather than new-city launches.
- [Waymo reportedly plans to quintuple robotaxi fleet to 20,000 vehicles by end of 2027](https://www.gvwire.com/2026/10/19/waymo-plans-to-quintuple-fleet-to-20000-semafor-reports/) — Source: Reuters (via GV Wire), citing a Semafor report; published 2026-10-19. Unnamed sources say Waymo aims to roughly double its fleet to 8,000 vehicles by end-2026 and reach 20,000 by end-2027, funded partly by the $5B debt financing it closed in early October; not a company-confirmed statement, so treat as a secondary-source signal of planned scale.
- [Aurora reports Q3 2026 results: $3M revenue, $234M net loss](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001828108/000182810826000094/aur-20260930.htm) — Source: SEC Form 10-Q; published 2026-10-28. Aurora's quarterly revenue rose to $3M (from $1M a year earlier) on higher utilization and expanded driverless trucking lanes, while R&D spend grew 13% to $203M and net loss widened to $234M (from $201M) — concrete evidence of how slowly commercial revenue is scaling relative to Aurora's public 2030 target of 30,000 driverless trucks.

## Maps & Navigation
- [HERE Technologies Expands Access to HERE Location Reasoning (HLR) With AWS](https://www.here.com/about/press-releases/here-technologies-expands-access-to-here-location-reasoning-hlr-delivering-geospatial-intelligence-for-real-world-ai-with-aws) — Source: HERE Technologies press release; published 2026-10-14. HERE broadened access to its Location Reasoning MCP server (map data, live traffic, geocoding, route optimization, EV charging intelligence) via AWS Marketplace pay-as-you-go pricing, AWS's Kiro agentic IDE, and an Agent Plugin compatible with Claude Code, Codex, VS Code, and Cursor, under its existing 10-year, $1B AWS cloud agreement; no latency or accuracy figures disclosed.

## Traffic Data & Open Data
- [Overture Maps rebuilds its schema as a code-generated model](https://docs.overturemaps.org/blog/2026/10/06/cng-workshop/) — Source: Overture Maps Foundation blog; published 2026-10-06. Overture's v2.0.0 schema is now a Python/Pydantic library rather than hand-written YAML, so JSON Schema, reference docs, and validation checks are auto-generated from one source model — reduces schema drift for researchers building on Overture's open building/transportation/places data.
- [Beyond the Parking Space: How Technology Is Transforming Curb Management](https://inrix.com/blog/beyond-the-parking-space-how-technology-is-transforming-curb-management/) — Source: INRIX blog; published 2026-10-22. INRIX describes digitized curb inventories plus real-time/camera-based occupancy data enabling automated loading-zone and AV/delivery-robot curb access, citing deployments with double parking cut by over 95% and turnover up roughly 70%; no cities or methodology given, so figures are vendor-reported and not independently verified.
