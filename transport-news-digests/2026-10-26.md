## Maps & Navigation
- [TomTom and DeepRoute.ai Partner to Advance AI-Based Automated Driving](https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/228849211236765/tomtom-and-deeproute-ai-partner-to-advance-ai-based-automated-driving/) — Source: TomTom newsroom; published 2026-10-12. TomTom signed an MoU with China's DeepRoute.ai to integrate TomTom's location data (road layouts, local rules, regional context) into DeepRoute.ai's automated-driving stack for advanced L2+/L2++ driver assistance, aimed at helping automakers scale ADAS across markets with differing traffic rules and validation requirements; a demo vehicle is being shown on open roads in Paris.
- [TomTom Expands EV Suite to Accelerate Deployment for Automakers and Deliver More Trusted Journeys for Drivers](https://www.tomtom.com/newsroom/press-releases/general/229787857507260/tomtom-expands-ev-suite-to-accelerate-deployment-for-automakers-and-deliver-more-trusted-journeys-for-drivers/) — Source: TomTom newsroom; published 2026-10-13. TomTom launched Electric Vehicle Management, a self-service platform letting automakers register EV variants (battery size, connectors, dimensions) for a unique digital ID that cuts onboarding "from months to minutes," paired with an AI consumption model blending physics-based and real-world driving data for range/charging predictions and routing across 2.3M+ connectors in 117 countries; the suite now supports over 6 million EVs.
