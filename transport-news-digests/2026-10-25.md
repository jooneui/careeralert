## Research Evidence
- [Agentic discovery of interpretable car-following laws from multi-source trajectory data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27836) — Source: arXiv (preprint, not peer-reviewed); published 2026-10-20; Jiamei Xu, Jie Sun, Keshuang Tang, Jian Sun. Data: NGSIM US-101, highD, and exiD trajectories for model discovery; Shanghai naturalistic driving data, NGSIM I-80, and Waymo Open Motion trajectories for held-out evaluation. Method: LLM-agent-guided hypothesis generation plus evolutionary symbolic regression under physics constraints, yielding a car-following model (AERM) combining a state-dependent relative-speed response with a spacing-dependent equilibrium-speed restoration term. Finding: AERM cuts spacing-prediction RMSE by 9.11%, 7.59%, and 21.73% versus the strongest classical/ML baseline on the three held-out real-world datasets. Limitation: the improvement shrinks under dataset-level shared parameters rather than per-trajectory calibration.
