## Maps & Navigation
- [Mapbox Geocoding Entrance Data Expands to Europe](https://www.mapbox.com/blog/mapbox-geocoding-entrance-data-expands-to-europe) — Source: Mapbox blog; published 2026-10-27. Mapbox added building-entrance coordinates (via an `entrances=true` parameter returning an "entrance" entry in `routable_points`) to its Geocoding API across 19 European countries and 100M+ addresses, extending prior US/Canada coverage so delivery and rideshare apps can route drivers to the correct door rather than just the building footprint; the feature is in public preview.

## Research Evidence
- [Behavioral Safety Assessment towards Large-scale Deployment of Autonomous Vehicles, Part II: Assessment Results](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08458) — Source: arXiv (preprint, not peer-reviewed); published 2026-10-06 (v2 2026-10-11); Henry X. Liu, Tinghan Wang, Xintao Yan, Haowei Sun, Zhijie Qiao, Kenneth Boyd, Shuo Feng, Greg Stevens, Greg McGuire (Mcity, University of Michigan). Data: physical test-track trials plus simulation of the open-source Level-4 stack Autoware.Universe at Mcity. Method: third-party behavioral-competency testing framework applied to a real automated-driving software stack rather than pure simulation. Finding: Autoware.Universe exhibited only 6 of 14 target behavioral competencies and a crash rate of about 3.01×10⁻³ crashes per mile, roughly 1,000x the average human-driver rate, with previously unknown unsafe scenarios uncovered. Limitation: tests a single open-source stack on a closed test track/sim, not a commercially deployed public-road AV fleet, so results may not generalize to production systems like Waymo's.
