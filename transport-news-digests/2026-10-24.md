## Research Evidence
- [Multiyear Pattern Discovery and Policy Implications of Transit Bus Involved Fatal Crashes](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42421-026-00180-6) — Source: Data Science for Transportation (peer-reviewed); published 2026-10-15; Tausif Islam Chowdhury, Sharif Ahmed Rafat, Subasish Das (Texas A&M safety-analytics group). Data: multiyear FARS fatal-crash records involving transit buses. Method: Cluster Correspondence Analysis to identify recurring crash-pattern groups. Finding: fatal transit-bus crashes cluster around intersection-related conflicts, high-speed roadways, non-motorist involvement, and inadequate traffic control, with unprotected medians and missing traffic-control devices raising severity. Limitation: available abstract text gives pattern groupings rather than effect-size percentages for each factor.
