# Economy & Markets — 2026-10-23

## Central Banks & Rates
- [Fed raises rates to 3.75%–4.00%; September minutes show most officials favor another hike by year-end](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20260916.htm) — Source: Federal Reserve; published 2026-10-07. Minutes of the September 15–16 meeting confirm a unanimous 12–0 vote to raise the target range a quarter point, with most participants saying another increase would likely be appropriate before year-end depending on incoming data.
- [Cleveland Fed's Hammack says more tightening is needed to cool demand, keeps open mind on October timing](https://investinglive.com/central-banks/fed-s-hammack-says-more-tightening-needed-to-cool-demand-keeps-open-mind-on-pace/) — Source: InvestingLive; published 2026-10-12. Hammack cited AI-driven corporate investment and wealth-fueled consumer spending keeping inflation in a 2.5%–3.0% range and warned of an emerging "inflationary psychology," while saying she will enter the Oct. 27–28 FOMC meeting with an open mind on timing.
- [Bank of Korea holds base rate at 3.00% after back-to-back hikes, Governor Shin signals more tightening ahead](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/10/22/bank-of-korea-holds-rate-at-3-percent-after-back-to-back) — Source: Seoul Economic Daily; published 2026-10-22. All seven board members voted unanimously to pause after July and August quarter-point hikes; Shin said the BOK "will respond until we are confident that inflation is stably reaching" its 2% target, with inflation projected at 2.7% this year.

## Inflation & Jobs
- [September CPI rises 3.7% year-on-year as gasoline prices jump 9.1%, hotter than expected](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_10142026.htm) — Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics; published 2026-10-14. Headline CPI rose 0.6% on the month and core CPI rose 0.3%, lifting the annual core rate to 2.6% from 2.4%; gasoline accounted for over half of the monthly increase and energy overall rose 5.2%.
- [September payrolls rise just 29,000 as unemployment ticks up to 4.2%, prior two months revised down a combined 60,000](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_10022026.htm) — Source: Bureau of Labor Statistics; published 2026-10-02. Payroll growth badly missed the roughly 90,000 consensus and July was revised from +21,000 to -10,000; average hourly earnings rose just 0.1% on the month and 3.0% year-on-year, the slowest annual pace since around 2021.

## Markets & Investment
- [Hot CPI print sends stocks, Treasury yields and the dollar reeling in broadest selloff in months](https://www.investing.com/news/economic-indicators/hot-september-cpi-sends-ratehike-bets-soaring-4949438) — Source: Reuters via Investing.com; published 2026-10-14. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq fell sharply on the inflation surprise, the 10-year Treasury yield jumped 9 basis points to 5.30%, the dollar index hit an 18-month high near 102.75, and gold fell 1.6% to about $4,088/oz as traders raised the odds of the Fed holding (rather than cutting) to about 49% from 19% before the report.
- [Gold slips to around $4,121/oz as rising oil prices stoke inflation worries ahead of the Fed meeting](https://depor.com/en/us-local/gold-price-today-october-20-2026-price-per-ounce-and-gram-in-usd-nnda-nnrt-noticia/) — Source: Reuters data via Depor; published 2026-10-20. Gold extended its pullback from January's record near $5,318/oz as crude prices climbed, feeding worries that energy costs will keep headline inflation elevated into the Oct. 27–28 FOMC decision.

## Global Economy & Currencies
- [South Korea's exports double in first 20 days of October to a record $61.6 billion on chip demand](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/economy/20261021/exports-double-in-1st-20-days-of-october-on-robust-chip-shipments) — Source: Yonhap via The Korea Times; published 2026-10-21. Semiconductor shipments rose 222.5% to $27.5 billion, 44.7% of total exports, pushing the trade surplus to $14.5 billion for the period and the year-to-date surplus to $266.9 billion.
- [China's economy grows 4.5% in the third quarter, beating forecasts on industrial output and retail sales](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/article/641311) — Source: China Daily; published 2026-10-19. Growth accelerated from 4.3% in Q2, keeping nine-month growth at 4.6% within Beijing's 4.5%-5% full-year target, even as fixed-asset investment stayed down 7.2% and the property slump continued.
- [KOSPI plunges 7.6% and triggers a circuit breaker as chip stocks follow Wall Street's inflation-driven selloff](https://kfgo.com/?p=1363839) — Source: Reuters via KFGO; published 2026-10-14. South Korea's benchmark fell to a six-month low in its seventh straight losing session, with Samsung Electronics down 9.5% and SK Hynix down 11.1%, in the index's 10th circuit-breaker activation of the year amid sticky US inflation data and doubts about the global AI investment boom.
