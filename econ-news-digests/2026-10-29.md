## Central Banks & Rates
- [FOMC holds federal funds rate at 3.75%–4.00% in a 10-2 split vote](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20261028a.htm) — Source: Federal Reserve; published 2026-10-28. - [Fed votes in split decision to hold rates steady despite sticky inflation](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/article/fed-votes-in-split-decision-to-hold-interest-rates-steady-despite-sticky-inflation-180108960.html) — Source: Yahoo Finance; published 2026-10-28. Chair Kevin Warsh said tightening is "starting to work some" but there are "no firm conclusions" and politics "played no role" in the decision; he also confirmed the Fed will trim its 2027 meeting schedule, and CME FedWatch priced an 86% chance of a quarter-point hike at the December 9 meeting.

- [Wall Street edges higher as Fed holds rates; chipmakers gain on SK hynix results](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026102905313419654) — Source: Asia Business Daily; published 2026-10-29.
