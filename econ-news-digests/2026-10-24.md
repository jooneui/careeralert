## Inflation & Jobs
- [Final October consumer sentiment holds near a record low at 48.0 as inflation expectations climb](https://spectrumlocalnews.com/us/snplus/politics/2026/10/23/consumer-sentiment-lingers-near-record-low-in-october-2026) — Source: University of Michigan Surveys of Consumers, via Spectrum News; published 2026-10-23.
