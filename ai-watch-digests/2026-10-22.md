New speech-to-text/text-to-speech model family plus a real-time voice-agent SDK (sub-500ms transcription latency, native end-of-turn prediction) aimed at voice agents; license and most parameter sizes are unstated (only Mini Transcribe's 4B is given), and "state-of-the-art" claims against Whisper/Scribe/Gemini are Mistral's own evals, not independent benchmarks.

## Community Views
- [Claude Duet (Show HN)](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50186037) — Source: Hacker News; community opinion (2026-10-21): commenters praised concrete personal-assistant wins (e.g., a tax audit catching an ~$8,000 error) and the strength of the underlying Opus 5.5 model, while the main criticism was clunky signup/integration (the waitlist re-asks for info the user already gave) plus privacy concerns and unclear added value over existing agent/automation workflows.
- [WorldTUI: I trained a 360M model to pretend to be a terminal](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50190363) — Source: Hacker News; community opinion (2026-10-21): commenters called the fine-tuned terminal-simulating model "very cool" and floated honeypot/art uses, while critics noted the author's own admission it "isn't really" useful, reported state bugs (created folders missing from `ls`), and questioned running a nondeterministic GPU model for a task a CPU program already handles reliably.
- [Using LLM for confidence scoring is a bad idea](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50176082) — Source: Hacker News; community opinion (2026-10-22): commenters largely agreed confidence scores need outcome calibration that LLMs don't inherently provide and that 0-100 self-rated scales are unreliable, while critics countered that the post offered no measurements of its own and that logprob-based or fine-tuned calibration methods can still yield usable confidence signals.
