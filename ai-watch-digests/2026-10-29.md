## Local LLMs
- [LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B](https://huggingface.co/LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B) — Source: model card (Hugging Face); LFM Open License v1.0 (free under $10M company revenue per secondary reporting), ~1.73B params, 256K combined input/output context, runs ~1GB RAM at 4-bit quantization with GGUF/MLX/ONNX/FP8/NVFP4 builds for edge devices (phone, laptop, Apple Silicon); card itself says it is tuned for local agents/tool-calling/structured output and explicitly not recommended for agentic coding or complex multi-step reasoning.

## Community Views
- [Gemini 4 Carbon](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50276792) — Source: Hacker News discussing Google's launch post, 2026-10-28 (965 points, 771 comments). Community opinion: some praised Carbon's benchmark spread (reportedly ahead on 15 of 17 benchmarks) and found it a solid daily driver for coding/prose via third-party tools, while others criticized confusing access/plan gating, tight usage quotas that throttled after a few exchanges, muddled model-naming, and suspicion that the benchmark announcement preceded wide availability for business reasons.
- [Claude Code regularly sends each background agent's transcript to its model](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50284255) — Source: Hacker News discussing a published gist, 2026-10-29 (475 points, 189 comments). Community opinion: the gist's author measured background-agent "status line" summary calls at ~16-20% of API spend/requests with no user-facing setting to disable them; an Anthropic engineer replied that the real-world cost share is closer to 2-6% and that a cache-miss fix (2.1.311) and a cheaper summarization mode are in progress, while critics said any undocumented token cost for a cosmetic status line is unjustified and called for an explicit opt-out.
